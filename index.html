Welcome to Canaan Wood

Canaan Wood is a professional wood board manufacturer and exporter based in China, a leading wood processing base. We specialize in plywood, solid wood boards, construction timber and wood pallet lumber. Every shipment goes through strict moisture and strength inspection before packing.

With years of export experience, we serve customers in Europe, Southeast Asia, the Middle East and South America. We offer customized sizes, thickness and MOQ as low as 1 x 20GP container. Contact us for a full catalog and quotation.
